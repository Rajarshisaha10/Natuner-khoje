About Chakdah Natuner-Khoje

Welcome to Chakdah Natuner-Khoje, recognized as the best NGO in India, a Government Registered Non-Government Organisation, started its journey in 2021, has been relentlessly working for the upliftment of the underprivileged people of the society, in the States of West Bengal and Jharkhand. The organization is registered under West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961 bearing Registration No. S0028930 of 2022-23. Our organisation also possesses CSR1 Certificate approved by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India.
