Our Activities

OUR PRESENT ACTIVITIES

Through our nine centres located at Uttardanga, Ranipur and Chhoto Mollarkhali in the District of South 24 Parganas; Sahebdanga, Mahuldanga and Gopal Nagar in the District of Bankura; Simni in the District of Purulia; Khairagachhi in the District of Murshidabad in West Bengal and Lakshmijol in the District of Pakur in Jharkhand, we have been providing free education to the students upto 8th standard who do not have access to quality education due to their financial condition. At the end of classes every day, they are provided with food to supplement their nutritional requirements.

Members and Well Wishers of the organization often celebrate their special occasions with the children of these centres.

Out of nine centres, two viz. Ranipur and Lakshmijol have been running in buildings constructed on our own land.

Through our four Charitable Homeo Dispensaries located at Purulia, Bankura, Ranipur and Chhoto Mollakhali centres, we have been providing free treatment alongwith medicines.

In order to promote sports and games among the children, we have been holding two gender neutral football coaching centres at Purulia and Murshidabad. These centres have been regularly producing footballers to play at higher levels.

With the objective of empowering women and to make them self-reliant, we have made available sewing machines at our Bankura, Ranipur, Murshidabad and Jharkhand centres where village women stitch garments and sell them in the local markets. They are also provided with lentils to make papads, bori etc..

We have also arranged drinking water for the villagers of Ranipur in Sunderbans through deep tubewell facility where they had to walk miles to fetch potable water.

To overcome the challenge of paucity of quality teachers at local level to teach higher classes, our members have come forward to hold online classes which have become very successful. The villagers are overwhelmed by this initiative of the organisation.

OUR FUTURE PROGRAMMES

  • To facilitate higher education to the students of our centres.
  • To construct school building at Khairagachhi, Murshidabad on a piece of land in our possession.
  • To acquire land at other locations for construction of school buildings.
  • To install solar power plants in the villages where our centres are located.
  • To hold regular free health camps with specialist doctors for the villagers.
