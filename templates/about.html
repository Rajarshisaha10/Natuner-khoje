About Us

Chakdah Natuner Khoje Official Website

Founded in 2021, Chakdah Natuner Khoje began as a grassroots relief initiative during the aftermath of the devastating Cyclone Yaas that affected the Sundarbans region. What started as emergency relief work evolved into a long-term mission to support marginalized communities through sustainable development programs. Today, the organization is supported by more than 350 members and well-wishers working toward social change.

The organization is registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961 with Registration No. S0028930 of 2022–23. Regestered under 12A & Approved under Section 80G of Income Tac Act 1961, as well as holds a CSR-1 Certificate approved by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India

Vision

To create an inclusive society where every individual has access to quality education, healthcare, nutrition, and opportunities for self-reliance regardless of their economic background.

Mission

  • Provide free education to children from financially weaker families.
  • Improve healthcare accessibility through charitable medical services.
  • Promote women's empowerment through livelihood programs.
  • Support nutrition and food security for vulnerable communities.
  • Develop rural infrastructure and sustainable village development.
  • Encourage sports, leadership, and talent development among children.

Key Impact Areas

Education for all

The NGO operates multiple educational centres across West Bengal as well as Jharkhand, providing free education up to Class 8 for children who lack access to quality Education. Students are also provided food support after classes to help address nutritional needs.

Free Healthcare Services

Through charitable homeopathy dispensaries in several districts, free medical consultations and medicines are provided to underserved communities.

Women Empowerment

The organization supports women through self-reliance initiatives including sewing training and garment production, helping them earn independent income.

Sports Development

Football coaching centres are run to encourage sports participation and help talented children pursue higher-level opportunities in athletics.

Rural Development

Projects include drinking water facilities, educational infrastructure development, and plans for solar-powered community centres in rural villages.

Current Operational Areas

The organization currently works across:

  • South 24 Parganas (Sundarbans Region)
  • Bankura
  • Purulia
  • Murshidabad
  • Nadia
  • Pakur (Jharkhand)

It operates educational, healthcare, and community welfare centres in these regions.
